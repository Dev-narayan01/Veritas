Archaeology — sample note 10

Archaeological survey maps what survives rather than what was used. A field that produced pottery may have held a settlement, or it may have been the place where broken pots were thrown, and distinguishing the two depends on careful recording of position.

A useful automation project starts by measuring the tasks that consume the most staff time. Loan renewal and fine collection are usually the first candidates, because both follow predictable rules and produce frequent small errors when handled by hand.
