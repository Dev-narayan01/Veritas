Manual Work Ergonomics — sample note 24

Repetitive manual work produces injury slowly and invisibly. Discomfort at the end of a shift is easy to dismiss, yet it is the earliest signal that posture or tool height is wrong, and adjusting the workstation early costs far less than treatment later.

Vocabulary is forgotten quickly without encounters in context. A word met once in a list and once in a novel is remembered far better than a word met three times in the same list, because the different contexts provide separate retrieval routes.
