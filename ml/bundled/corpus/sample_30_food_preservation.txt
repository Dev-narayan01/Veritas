Food Preservation — sample note 30

Drying, salting and fermenting all work by making the environment hostile to the microorganisms that spoil food. The methods differ in which variable they change, but the underlying principle is the same, and mixing them poorly is the usual cause of failure.

Students often confuse familiarity with understanding. A chapter that feels easy on the second read may still be impossible to explain aloud, and explaining aloud is closer to what an examination actually asks for.
