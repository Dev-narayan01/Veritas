Sleep Research — sample note 20

Sleep studies distinguish between time in bed and time asleep, and the difference is often the most informative measurement. People who report insomnia frequently spend long periods awake in bed, which reinforces the association between the bed and wakefulness.

Improving reliability is often cheaper than increasing frequency. Signal priority at two junctions and a realistic timetable can remove most of the delay that passengers notice, whereas an extra vehicle costs money every day of the year.
