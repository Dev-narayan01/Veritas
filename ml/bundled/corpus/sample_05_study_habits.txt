Study Habits — sample note 05

Spaced practice outperforms rereading for almost every kind of material. The gain comes from retrieving information when it feels slightly difficult, which is why a quiz taken a week later teaches more than a quiz taken immediately.

Breaks are more effective when they are frequent and short. A five minute pause every hour reduces strain more than a single long break, because recovery happens before fatigue accumulates rather than after it has already reduced control and attention.
