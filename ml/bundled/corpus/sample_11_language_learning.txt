Language Learning — sample note 11

Comprehensible input drives language learning more reliably than memorising grammar rules. Learners who read and listen extensively build a feel for what sounds correct, and that feel supports production long before explicit rules can be recalled quickly.

Home preservation fails most often because a recipe is scaled up without adjusting time. A larger batch needs longer to reach the required temperature, and the recipe that worked for two kilograms may quietly under-process five.
