Renewable Energy — sample note 07

Solar output depends on panel angle, air temperature and dust. A panel that is perfectly aligned in winter may be badly aligned in summer, and a single dusty week can erase the benefit of a careful installation plan.

Dating a site depends on several independent methods agreeing. Radiocarbon dates, stratigraphy and stylistic comparison of finds each carry uncertainty, and reporting a single number without that uncertainty misrepresents the evidence.
